### **7. Difference Between AI and ML**

1. Compare AI and ML based on:

| **Criteria**        | **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**                                                                             | **Machine Learning (ML)**                                                                                                           |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**      | AI is the broader concept of machines being able to carry out tasks in a way that mimics human intelligence. | ML is a subset of AI that focuses on enabling systems to learn from data and improve over time without being explicitly programmed. |
| **Scope**           | Broad – includes reasoning, problem-solving, perception, planning, language understanding, etc.              | Narrower – limited to learning from data and making predictions or decisions based on that data.                                    |
| **Data Dependency** | May or may not rely heavily on data. Rule-based systems are possible without large datasets.                 | Highly data-dependent – requires large amounts of quality data for training models.                                                 |
| **Examples**        | Chatbots, autonomous vehicles, expert systems, robotics, natural language processing                         | Spam filters, recommendation systems, image recognition, fraud detection                                                            |
| **Goals**           | To simulate human intelligence and behavior.                                                                 | To enable systems to automatically learn and improve from experience.                                                               |
